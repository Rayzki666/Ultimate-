# Frame: shoot toward a style

The primary journey is Styles → choose a look → open Camera → follow one direction → capture → save in Photos.

Four initial presets change actual guidance: Cinematic Portrait, Golden Glow, Travel Story and Editorial. Each supplies default framing, composition and subject-size ranges. Severe exposure errors, missing people and cut joints always take priority. Golden Glow changes the backlight action; environmental notes react to measured warmth, brightness distribution and upper/lower contrast. Readiness resets when a preset changes.

The English interface has four tabs: Camera, Styles, Photos, Settings. Conversation scripts and the old recipes/profile content are no longer mounted. Existing stored data is preserved, and legacy saved tab names migrate safely.

Preset artwork is illustrative, not a promise of an automatic photographic effect. No filters are applied. Do not claim background-object understanding, sunset detection, expression recognition or autofocus measurements from pixel statistics.

Next phase: explicitly enabled visual scene understanding could identify a cluttered background or a better angle from current frames. Such responses must be tied to their frame timestamp and invalidated after movement. Real-device validation should precede continuous cloud sampling or claims of camera performance.

Acceptance: selecting a style must change live advice, persist on reload, remain overridable, preserve safety/quality priorities, keep saving reachable on small screens and expose English text throughout visible flows.
