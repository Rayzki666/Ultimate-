About the guidance

Guidance adapts to measured light and subject position. Style suggestions are shooting directions, not filters. Basic checks cover visible head and shoulders (face landmarks in Close-up), light, framing and stability. Poses are flexible. Optional cloud AI considers the scene and style; cloud AI checks whether visible eyes look open in its sampled frame; local checks watch face detail and facial changes. These are fallible heuristics, not precise focus or beauty scores. Full AI readiness also needs angle access. Slow, missing or outdated analysis never triggers the AI signal.
